# Rise Above: My Activity & Adventure Workbook
### A companion for young readers (ages 6–10)

Read a chapter of *Rise Above* with a grown-up, then turn to this page and have fun with it!

---

## Chapter 1: A Village by the River

**What happened:** Tommy the tortoise and Ronny the rabbit lived in the same village but never really *saw* each other.

**Word of the day:** STEADY — moving the same calm way every time.

**Draw it:** Draw Herbville! Don't forget Lazy River, Mount Riser, and the market square.

**Think about it:** Who is someone at your school or home who is very different from you? Write or draw one good thing about them.

---

## Chapter 2: The Challenge

**What happened:** Ronny knocked Tommy over and didn't say sorry. Tommy challenged him to a race — and won, because Ronny stopped to nap!

**Word of the day:** FOCUS — keeping your mind on one thing.

**Circle one:** Who finished the race first?
🐢 Tommy &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 🐇 Ronny

**Try it:** Pick one small job (tidy your room, finish your homework) and do it slowly and steadily from start to finish, without stopping. How did it feel?

---

## Chapter 3: The Bitter Defeat

**What happened:** Ronny felt embarrassed about losing. Instead of staying angry, he asked himself *why* he lost, and figured out it was because he got too confident.

**Word of the day:** REFLECT — to think carefully about what happened.

**Fill in the blank:** Next time I lose a game or make a mistake, instead of getting upset, I will ask myself: "Why did that happen, and what can I learn?" ___________________________

**Draw it:** Draw a picture of Ronny lying awake thinking. What do you think is in his thought bubble?

---

## Chapter 4: The Rematch

**What happened:** Ronny apologized for being rude, trained his focus, and raced again. This time he won fair and square — no shortcuts, just real effort.

**Word of the day:** COMEBACK — trying again after a loss, and doing better.

**Checklist — Ronny's Comeback Plan:**
- [ ] Said he was sorry
- [ ] Asked for a fair rematch
- [ ] Stayed focused the whole race
- [ ] Didn't stop for a nap!

**Try it:** Think of something you didn't do well at the first time. What is ONE thing you could change to do better next time?

---

## Chapter 5: Finding the Edge

**What happened:** Tommy felt sad that Ronny was faster than him. But instead of giving up, he thought about what HE was good at — swimming! — and raced across the river instead.

**Word of the day:** STRENGTH — something you're naturally good at.

**My Strengths page:** Draw yourself in the middle of the page. Around you, draw 3 things you are good at (maybe drawing, kindness, building, singing, helping others, or something else!).

**Think about it:** Tommy didn't try to be fast like Ronny. He used what made HIM special instead. What makes YOU special?

---

## Chapter 6: Smelling the Roses

**What happened:** Ronny sat quietly by the river (instead of rushing around) and noticed a floating log. That gave him an idea for how to cross the water!

**Word of the day:** NOTICE — to really pay attention to the world around you.

**Quiet Minute Challenge:** Sit somewhere for one whole minute without your phone or any toys. Just look around. Write or draw ONE thing you noticed that you never noticed before.

---

## Chapter 7: Inventive Cycles

**What happened:** Ronny didn't stop at one good idea — he asked the villagers what they needed, then tried, tested, and improved his river-crossing invention again and again until it worked really well.

**Word of the day:** IMPROVE — to make something better, a little bit at a time.

**Build it:** Draw your own invention that could help your family or your class with something tricky. What problem does it solve?

---

## Chapter 8: Water Under the Bridge

**What happened:** Ronny said a real, honest sorry to Tommy. They became true friends, and when they raced as a TEAM — Ronny running, Tommy swimming — they were faster together than either was alone!

**Word of the day:** TEAMWORK — working together so everyone's strengths help each other.

**Match it:** Draw a line connecting each animal to their superpower in the team race.
Ronny — running fast
Tommy — swimming strong

**Try it:** Think of a time working WITH someone made something easier or more fun than doing it alone. Draw it!

---

## Chapter 9: The Outcast

**What happened:** Fiona the falcon was new in town and wanted to join a team, but lots of animals said unkind things and turned her away. She felt very sad and alone — until Tommy and Ronny welcomed her.

**Word of the day:** KIND — being friendly and welcoming, especially to someone new or different.

**Think about it:** Has anyone ever made you feel left out? How did it feel? Draw a picture of you welcoming someone new to your group.

**My promise:** I promise that if I see someone who looks lonely or left out, I will _____________________.

---

## Chapter 10: Day of Reckoning

**What happened:** During the big relay race, Fiona started worrying about beating the other animals instead of just flying her best. When she remembered to focus only on her own goal, she flew better than ever and won!

**Word of the day:** GOAL — the thing you are trying to reach.

**Color it:** Draw Fiona soaring above the mountain with the finish line in sight. What is she looking at?

**Try it:** Next time you're doing something hard (a test, a race, a tricky puzzle), try looking only at YOUR goal instead of worrying about everyone else.

---

## Chapter 11: Flying High

**What happened:** Fiona raced again, this time alone, and almost let mean words stop her. But she remembered "I am built for the sky," flew up above the chaos, and won the whole race by being exactly who she was.

**Word of the day:** YOURSELF — the one-of-a-kind person only you can be.

**My "Built For" page:** Finish this sentence and draw a picture of it: "I am built for ______________________."
